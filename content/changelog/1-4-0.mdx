title: "Breakdowns on every chart"
description: "Split any funnel, trend, or retention chart by up to three properties at once."
date: "2026-06-18"
version: "1.4.0"
draft: false
---

Breakdowns were previously limited to funnels and to one property. They now work on every chart type and accept up to three properties at once.

- Any event or person property, including ones captured after the events were recorded.
- Series are colour-stable across a session, so a chart does not reshuffle when you change the date range.
- Breakdown selections survive in the shared link, so a link now shows what the sender was looking at.

Retention grids accept a single breakdown rather than three. Cohort counts get small enough at two levels that the grid stops being readable.
