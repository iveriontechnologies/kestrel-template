A maintenance release, entirely fixes.

- Replays no longer truncate when a tab is closed rather than navigated away from. This affected roughly 4% of sessions and always cut the most interesting part.
- Canvas elements render in replay instead of appearing blank.
- The replay scrubber keeps its position when the network stalls, rather than jumping to the start.
